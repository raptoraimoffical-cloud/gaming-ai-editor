# Reference-based simultaneous activity

Inspected all four supplied local reference videos, using their complete contact sheets and selected full-resolution frames. Their content is analysis material, not instructions embedded in a video. The reusable grammar is recorded in `electron/reference-activity.mjs` and passed to the trained editorial, creative-action and local-animation planners.

The references combine a continuing lead, changing supporting pictures or readable source evidence, and a compatible actor response or action. The Diabolical strategy holds two characters with a move/ability screenshot between them; Champions assembles a named team and later combines a character, stats/ability evidence and a clock. Charizard keeps its lead on a continuing set while threats/weather appear, then changes the environment for the reversal. Sceptile keeps its ability description readable while the character and stat-rise/electric response move. Uploaded battle proof and a final payoff can hold longer.

Changes enforce that grammar in the renderer pipeline:

- Plan up to six compatible recipes per passage instead of one small gag or a maximum of three outputs. The prompt considers alternatives and asks for support, interaction and payoff across the whole passage, usually with meaningful changes around every 1–2 seconds.
- A chart or source information card no longer disables separate character/prop activity. Card lanes are measured before motion is authored. Independent prop/picture actions can coexist, including accessories following an actor through its performance. Two competing movements on one actor are rejected and reported.
- Compact recipes anchor on the actual prop noun or action verb within the quoted narration. A whole-sentence quote cannot show a crown at “on paper” or launch a prop at “then” before the narrated fall. New reveals have no early preroll.
- A continuing lead can notice a supporting reveal while source evidence stays still. Real sourced rain, snow and confetti can move behind the cast. No fallback weather/prop image is manufactured. Original Google provider settings and caption appearance are retained.
- Sounds follow arrival, travel/contact and payoff. Authored phase cues can be closer together than generic accents; manual audio remains protected. Missing source images and skipped actions are disclosed.
- Pipeline evidence audits delivered simultaneous foreground pictures and quiet passages. Background patterns, captions and shadows do not satisfy the foreground-picture count. This is a composition check, not a viewer-retention estimate or a guarantee of creator-level output.

Verification includes automatic regression tests, a production build, rendered frame inspection, and an actual local Qwen3:8b planning run. It proposed three alternatives and four executable recipes: a plan picture, a confidence crown, a flying paper collapse and a simultaneous character reaction. The recording is in `workspace/layered-activity-check/trained-planning.json`; the recorded proposal was replayed through the corrected cue compiler and renderer. Controlled fixture and actual trained-model runs are reported separately. These diagnostics use synthetic words/timings and existing published artwork, not a finished user export or live Google search.

The target is to apply the references' visual storytelling to every Pokemon edit. Results still depend on correct narration recognition, suitable source assets, the configured model's proposals and any supplied gameplay. The application cannot recreate unseen battle footage or promise that every automatic joke equals a human creator's work. Quiet/manual/no-motion preferences and complete attacks remain supported, and captions are not restyled by the activity pass.
