# Acting out the narration

The trained creative planner can author twenty executable scene recipes rather
than relying on a generic reveal/wobble. New capabilities include feints/dodges,
chases, side-by-side partner formations, celebrations, slumps, taunts,
disbelieving shakes, bouncing/orbiting sourced props and hidden-picture reveals.
It receives the already-inspected four gold reference patterns, surrounding
narration and recently used ideas. Actions keep exact spoken cues, real resolved
actors and sourced original props. A dodge or partnership does not imply a hit
or receive a contact sound. No damage values, legality or outcomes are invented.

An executable planned actor interaction takes precedence over a smaller
automatic comic performance. When a description image temporarily reframes
the actor, its continuous visible span can now carry an authored performance
across that staging boundary instead of being rejected. The original frame is
restored at its actual clock time and a second copy is not left on screen.
Independent source pictures can accompany a complete attack; competing movement
on its fighters is deferred. Complete gameplay and manual objects retain their
protected intervals.

This is a larger cutout/prop choreography vocabulary and stronger planning
contract, not model-weight fine-tuning or a promise of creator-level humor or
viewer retention. Captions and source narration retain their appearance/timing.
